Before you install:

  • ArthurLegal is a legal research and drafting tool; it does not give legal advice. A
    lawyer must verify every output.
  • Arthur Mask pseudonymises; it does not anonymise. Responsibility under data protection
    law (such as the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), professional secrecy rules (such as Article 36
    of the Turkish Attorneys Act) and confidentiality agreements remains with the user.
  • Setup adds its local servers to the Claude Desktop configuration (arthurlegal-yerel,
    arthur-tapu; arthur-mask if installed; arthur-uyap if the UYAP bridge is present on this
    computer) and backs the configuration up before changing it.
  • Updates install in the background from the ArthurLegal releases on GitHub. Every update
    is signed with the ArthurLegal release key; a file whose signature does not verify is not
    installed.
  • The research tools send the search queries Claude writes to the ArthurLegal research
    server (arthurlegal-mcp.fly.dev). Do not put client names or confidential information
    into these queries.
  • The UYAP bridge (Turkey's court information system) is distributed separately. If it is
    installed, it is read-only: it sends nothing to UYAP and passes no data to the model
    without masking it.
