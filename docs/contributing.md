# Contributing workflows and playbook positions

[Türkçe](katki.md) · [Azərbaycan dili](tohfe.md)

ArthurLegal is free and open source for lawyers in Türkiye. The workflows on the desk and the contract playbooks can
grow from lawyers' shared know-how: you propose a workflow or a contract position, its statutory and case-law basis is
checked against the official source, and the workflow reaches everyone's desk.

## What you can propose

- **A new workflow:** the steps of a task you do often (e.g. a preliminary review for a rent determination case).
- **A playbook position:** for one issue in one contract type, the opening position, the must-have and the fallback.
- **A fix:** a missing, outdated or wrong step or article in an existing workflow.

## How

1. With your GitHub account, fill in the [proposal form](https://github.com/beerbottle90/ArthurLegal/issues/new?template=is-akisi-onerisi.yml).
2. Write the steps in order, including the information the lawyer must provide and the points where a decision is made.
3. Add the statutes and decisions the workflow relies on, with official links where possible.

## What is checked

- **Confidentiality:** proposals containing client names, case numbers, firm names or confidential information are not
  published. Use fictional or generalised examples.
- **Legal basis:** every article is read from the official text with `tr_mevzuat_madde_getir`; if what the proposal
  attributes to it (who holds the right, conditions, time limits, consequences) does not match the text, it is corrected
  or removed. Nothing that cannot be confirmed from an official source is added.
- **Professional limits:** workflows are tools for lawyers; proposals that give advice directly to the public, read as
  advertising or promise outcomes are not added.

## What happens next

An accepted proposal is added to the packages and to the Workflows screen on the desk and mentioned in the release notes.
If a proposal is declined or changed, the reason is written on the proposal page.
